Privacy policy

This page explains what information Flotix collects, why it is collected and how it is protected.

1. Information we collect

Account details such as name, email and role, together with vehicle, location, fuel and maintenance data reported by the web application and the IoT device.

2. How we use it

The data powers fleet management, fuel control, preventive maintenance and real time monitoring, and helps us improve the platform.

3. Sharing

Information is shared only inside the organization that owns the account and with affiliated workshops when a maintenance task requires it. It is never sold to third parties.

4. Security and retention

We apply access controls by role and keep data only as long as the account is active or as needed to provide the service.

5. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time by contacting the Flotix team.
