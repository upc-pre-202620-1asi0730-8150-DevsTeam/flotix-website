Terms and conditions

These terms describe how visitors and registered users may use the Flotix landing page, web application and IoT device.

1. Scope

These terms apply to the Flotix landing page, the web application and the companion GPS, odometer and fuel sensor device offered to fleet owners.

2. Accounts and roles

Every account belongs to one of three roles: owner, driver or workshop. Each role sees only the screens and data relevant to its work, and an owner may assign roles to the members of their organization.

3. Acceptable use

Users agree to provide accurate vehicle and account information, to use the IoT device only on vehicles they own or operate, and not to interfere with telemetry data belonging to another account.

4. Data and privacy

Location, fuel and maintenance data collected through the platform is used to power the features owners, drivers and workshops rely on, and is not sold to third parties.

5. Professional responsibility

The Flotix team follows recognized software engineering ethics guidance in how the platform is built, tested and deployed, and documents changes to the service through its public version control repository.
